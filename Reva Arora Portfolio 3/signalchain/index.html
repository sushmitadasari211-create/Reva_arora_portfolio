Why this project

A diagnosis, not a dashboard

Most funnel tools stop at counts and percentages. SignalChain isolates the single worst leak against an industry benchmark and explains why it is happening.

A ranked, costed fix

Every diagnosis ends in 3 ranked interventions with cost, time to implement, and estimated candidates recovered — a real tradeoff, not a suggestion box.

An artifact a leader would read

The Executive Summary tab produces the exact one-page document a TA leader would take into a staffing review.

Built browser-only with Tailwind, Chart.js, and Prism.js · No backend, no build step
