## Goal
Build a portfolio site for Reva Arora aimed at recruiters and hiring managers at Meta, Netflix, NVIDIA, Amazon, and Apple. The content has to meet big-tech hiring standards: clear, evidence-based, and results-first. Good visual design is expected, but substance comes first.

## Narrative standard
- Professional and precise. No filler adjectives or "passionate/innovative" clichés.
- Position her using the resume plus the project themes (analytics, AI/RAG, FinOps, security, supply chain) and describe her as a builder who turns data and AI into business outcomes.
- Every claim must trace back to the resume, a repo README, or a live demo. Don't invent metrics. If a number isn't available, mark it [METRIC NEEDED] and list it for me to confirm.

## Site structure
1. Home: headline, 2-3 line positioning statement, core skills, links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and the resume.
2. About/Experience: drawn from the resume.
3. One page per project (9 total), each with:
   - Business problem: who is affected and what it costs them
   - Proposed solution: approach, architecture, tech stack
   - Impact: quantified (%, $, time saved, accuracy) plus qualitative outcomes
   - Links: GitHub repo and live demo, both clickable
4. Contact: LinkedIn, GitHub, email.
